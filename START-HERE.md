# MetaScience course

## ▶ [Tap here: open the start page](http://localhost:4000/)

The start page opens in a new tab. Its big buttons open **OpenCode** (the AI assistant, already in your course
folder) and **RStudio**, connect your free AI account, and show whether the AI is ready.
Keep that tab open. You can always come back to this tab and tap the link again.

---

### The very first time

1. **Wait for the setup.** It runs by itself for about 15-25 minutes. Nothing to type. The start page opens right
   away and says when the setup is finished.
2. **A box may ask "Do you trust the authors of the files in this folder?"** Tap **Trust Folder & Continue**.
   GitHub asks this in every new browser; this course folder is safe.
3. **After the setup, connect a free OpenRouter account** (once, about 2 minutes, no card): on the start page, tap
   **Connect a free OpenRouter account**. Why: the built-in free AI counts its free use per internet connection, and a
   Codespace shares its connection with many other people, so it can run out early. With your own free account,
   OpenCode switches to it by itself when that happens and says so in the chat.

### If the link above does not open the start page

- Tap **PORTS** at the bottom of this screen, then the globe next to **Start page (tap here)**.
- Still nothing: menu (☰ at the top left) > **Terminal** > **New Terminal**, then paste
  `bash .devcontainer/start.sh` and press Enter. Wait a minute and tap the link again.
- This page closed? Tap **START-HERE.md** in the file list on the left.

### Good to know

- Your work stays here between sessions. Your course files are in the **MetaScience** folder in the file list on the
  left. To download a file you hand in: right-click (or press and hold) it there, then **Download**.
- The Codespace switches off by itself after 30 minutes without use. Nothing is lost; open it again from
  [github.com/codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces).
- It is deleted if you do not open it for 30 days, so download what you hand in.

### Money: all free

Never press **Subscribe** in OpenCode. Never add a credit card or payment method to GitHub, OpenRouter or any other
site. Without one, nothing can charge you: when the free hours or requests run out, things simply stop until the next
month or day. A "$0.00" shown on these sites is normal.

If your GitHub account **already** has a card: github.com/settings/billing > Budgets and alerts > New budget >
Product-level budget > Codespaces, amount $0, tick "Stop usage when budget limit is reached", Create budget.
