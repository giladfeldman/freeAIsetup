MetaScience course: your computer in the browser
================================================

If a box asks "Do you trust the authors of the files in this folder?", press "Trust Folder & Continue".
(GitHub asks this in every new browser; this course folder is safe.)

The first time, the setup runs by itself in the background for about 15-25 minutes (nothing to type).
It is finished when the file setup-report.txt appears in the MetaScience folder in the file list on
the left (/workspaces/MetaScience/setup-report.txt). Open it: every line should say OK.

Everything is then installed: OpenCode with free AI models, R, RStudio, Python, Quarto, Git and Node.js.
Your course folder is /workspaces/MetaScience

THE START PAGE: EVERYTHING WITH ONE TAP
  A box appears at the bottom right: "Your application running on port 4000 is available".
  Tap "Open in Browser" there: the start page opens in its own tab. Its big buttons open OpenCode
  (already in your course folder) and RStudio in new tabs, show whether the AI is ready, and restart
  OpenCode. Keep that tab open and come back to it any time.
  No box, or you closed it: tap PORTS at the bottom of this screen, then the globe next to
  "Start page (tap here)". (Not the preview inside this window: Firefox and iPads refuse to show it.)

  FIRST TIME: on the start page, tap "Connect a free OpenRouter account" (about 2 minutes, no card).
  Why: the built-in free AI counts its free use per internet connection, and a Codespace shares its
  connection with many other people, so it can run out early. With your own free account, OpenCode
  switches to it by itself when that happens and tells you so in the chat. Nothing else to do.
  (Without the start page: menu > Terminal > New Terminal, paste  bash setup-mac.sh --keys )

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your work stays here between sessions. Your course files are also in the MetaScience folder in the
    file list on the left. To download a file you hand in: right-click (or press and hold) it there,
    then Download.
  - The Codespace switches off by itself after 30 minutes without use. Nothing is lost; open it again
    from github.com/codespaces
  - It is deleted if you do not open it for 30 days, so download what you hand in.

MONEY: ALL FREE
  Never press "Subscribe" in OpenCode. Never add a credit card or payment method to GitHub, OpenRouter or any other site. Without one,
  nothing can charge you: when the free hours or requests run out, things simply stop until the
  next month or day. A "$0.00" shown on these sites is normal.
  If your GitHub account ALREADY has a card: github.com/settings/billing -> Budgets and alerts ->
  New budget -> Product-level budget -> Codespaces, amount $0, tick "Stop usage when budget limit
  is reached", Create budget.
