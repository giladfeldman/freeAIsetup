MetaScience course: your computer in the browser
================================================

If a box asks "Do you trust the authors of the files in this folder?", press "Trust Folder & Continue".
(GitHub asks this in every new browser; this course folder is safe.)

The first time, the setup runs for about 25 minutes in the TERMINAL panel at the bottom. Wait until it
shows the final summary. Its result is also saved in /workspaces/MetaScience/setup-report.txt.

Everything is then installed: OpenCode with free AI models, R, RStudio, Python, Quarto, Git and Node.js.
Your course folder is /workspaces/MetaScience

FREE AI KEY (needed in most Codespaces)
  OpenCode's built-in free models usually refuse Codespaces ("Free usage limit reached"; the setup
  report then says so). Add your own free key once, in the TERMINAL panel at the bottom:
      bash setup-mac.sh --keys
  It shows where to get a free key (for example OpenRouter or Groq, about 2 minutes, no payment)
  and then checks that the AI works.

OPEN THE PROGRAMS
  Tap PORTS at the bottom of this screen, then the globe icon next to a name:
  - OpenCode (AI assistant): port 4096. Choose the folder /workspaces/MetaScience
  - RStudio: port 8787
  If a tab does not open, allow pop-ups for github.dev, or press and hold the address in PORTS
  and open it in a new tab.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your work stays here between sessions. Your course files are also in the MetaScience folder in the
    file list on the left. To download a file you hand in: right-click (or press and hold) it there,
    then Download.
  - The Codespace switches off by itself after 30 minutes without use. Nothing is lost; open it again
    from github.com/codespaces
  - It is deleted if you do not open it for 30 days, so download what you hand in.

MONEY: ALL FREE
  Never add a credit card or payment method to GitHub, OpenRouter or any other site. Without one,
  nothing can charge you: when the free hours or requests run out, things simply stop until the
  next month or day. A "$0.00" shown on these sites is normal.
